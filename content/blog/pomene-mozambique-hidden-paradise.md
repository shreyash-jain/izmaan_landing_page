On their first morning in Pomene, most guests do the same thing. They walk down off the dune, look left along the beach, look right, and count the people. It doesn't take long. On an ordinary day the answer is somewhere between nobody and one fisherman.

That empty beach is the whole case for **Pomene, Mozambique**. It isn't really a secret. People have fished here for decades, and in summer a cruise ship calls at the bay. But most travellers on this coast drive straight past the turn-off on the EN1 between Tofo and Vilankulo. This post explains why they miss it, and whether you should take that turn.

## Key takeaways

- **What:** a sandy peninsula inside the **Pomene National Reserve**, established in 1964, in Inhambane Province.
- **Where:** about **106 km south of Vilankulo**, between the Indian Ocean and a tidal estuary.
- **Why it feels hidden:** no town, no resort strip, and the only road in ends in deep sand. **You need a 4×4.**
- **What to do:** snorkel, fish, watch whales (Jun–Nov) and nesting turtles (Oct–Mar), and paddle the mangroves.
- **Who it suits:** self-sufficient travellers who want quiet more than convenience.

## Where is Pomene, Mozambique?

Pomene is a narrow peninsula on the Inhambane coast of southern Mozambique, about 106 km south of Vilankulo. The open Indian Ocean runs along one side of it. A wide, shallow estuary fringed with mangroves lies on the other. All of it falls inside the Pomene National Reserve, which Mozambique's conservation authority [ANAC](https://anac.gov.mz/en/parques/pomene) records as established in 1964 in Massinga district.

### Why Pomene has stayed under the radar

Mostly, it's the road. You can drive on tar most of the way, but the last section into the reserve is soft sand, and that decides who arrives. Reserve status does the rest, because it keeps building to a minimum. There is no town centre, no row of beach bars and no supermarket. Everything you plan to eat comes in with you.

## What makes Pomene feel so remote

Start with the beach. It is long, wide and white, and from our dune it is a 100 m walk down to the sand. The water stays warm all year. The reef starts close enough to shore that many snorkellers simply swim out to it. Our [diving and snorkelling guide](/journal/diving-snorkelling-pomene) covers where to go and when.

Then there is everything behind the beach. ANAC lists vegetated dunes, savannah, mangroves and swamps inside the reserve, with flamingos and pelicans among the birdlife and a lighthouse on its highest point, 110 m up. On the point stand the ruins of the old Pomene Hotel, a colonial-era resort that never recovered from the [war years](https://theeastafrican.co.ke/tea/magazine/pomene-hotel-ruins-mozambique-5547348). Nearby, when the swell is running, the sea spouts through the blowholes.

At night there is very little artificial light. The stars come out properly, and in season so do the [nesting turtles](/journal/turtle-nesting-season-pomene).

## Things to do in Pomene

- **Snorkelling and diving** over reef just offshore.
- **Fishing.** Pomene's reef and game fishing is what first brought many South African families here.
- **Whale watching.** Humpbacks pass from roughly June to November, often close enough to [see from the dune](/journal/whale-watching-pomene-mozambique).
- **Turtle nesting**, October to March. Use red light only, keep your distance and never touch them.
- **The estuary and mangroves**, best explored by kayak on a rising tide.
- **The hotel ruins and the blowholes**, a morning's walk with a camera.

## Pomene vs the busier Mozambique coast

Tofo and Vilankulo are good places. They're just easier, and easier means busier.

| | Pomene | Tofo, Vilankulo and similar towns |
| --- | --- | --- |
| The road in | Deep sand at the end, 4×4 only | Tar to town |
| Food | Self-catering; bring supplies in | Restaurants, markets, shops |
| Evenings | Dark and quiet | Bars and restaurants |
| Activity operators | Few; arrange ahead | Several dive centres in town |
| People on the beach | Very few, except cruise days | Busier, especially in school holidays |

### The honest catch: cruise days

[MSC Cruises](https://www.msccruises.com/int/our-cruises/destinations/south-africa/mozambique/pomene) sells Pomene as a port of call on round trips from Durban during the South African summer, with its own beach and excursions for passengers. For a few hours on those days the bay is a different place. MSC publishes its sailing dates, so if total solitude is the point of your trip, check them against yours.

## Staying in Pomene: Izmaan Lodge

[Izmaan Lodge](/#lodge) is two timber-and-thatch units on the dune, with **four en-suite bedrooms** between them, all running on solar power. The full kitchen and thatched dining area make it a **self-catering** lodge first. Half-board and all-inclusive are available as add-ons with two weeks' notice. A couple can take one unit, and a family or group can [take the whole lodge for up to eight](/journal/family-holiday-mozambique-pomene).

The deck looks straight out over the bay, so in whale season breakfast often comes with blows on the horizon.

## When to visit and how to get there

![Infographic calendar showing the best time to visit Pomene, Mozambique: humpback whales June to November, turtle nesting October to March, cooler dry months May to September, warmer wetter months November to March, and fishing, diving and snorkelling all year](/images/blog/pomene-mozambique-hidden-paradise-calendar.svg)

There's no bad month for Pomene, only different ones. Our [month-by-month guide](/journal/best-time-to-visit-pomene) has the detail.

Most visitors fly into **Vilankulo (VNX)** and drive south. Others come all the way by road from South Africa through the [Komatipoort border](/journal/komatipoort-border-crossing-mozambique). Either way, the last stretch is sand, and you'll need a **high-clearance 4×4** with lowered tyre pressures. Some EN1 stretches are still being repaired after the 2025–26 floods, so **re-check the route before you leave**. Our [4×4 driving guide](/journal/drive-to-pomene-4x4-guide) explains the sand section.

Inhambane is a malaria area. See a travel clinic before you go.

## Is Pomene worth visiting?

Yes, if the list of things Pomene lacks sounds like a relief. It suits divers, anglers, couples, and families who would rather cook their own catch than queue at a buffet.

It isn't for you if you want nightlife, shops, restaurants or a drive with no sand in it. Being that far from everything is the price of the quiet, and it's real.
