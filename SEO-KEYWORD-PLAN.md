# Izmaan Lodge — SEO Keyword Plan & Content Calendar

**Prepared:** 10 July 2026 · **Cadence:** 3 posts/month · **Status: awaiting approval — no posts written yet**

How this was built: a multi-agent deep-research pass (5 search angles, 21 sources fetched, 80 claims
extracted, 25 adversarially verified) over real SA-overlander forums (4x4community.co.za, TripAdvisor
Mozambique forum, DriveMoz), competitor lodge sites, and the aggregator pages that currently rank for
Pomene terms — plus a code audit of this repo's blog engine.

**Honesty note on volumes:** no Ahrefs/Semrush data was available, so this plan contains **no monthly
search-volume numbers** — none were invented. Prioritisation is based on evidence of real demand
(actual forum threads, competitor pages targeting the term, autocomplete-style question phrasing)
and competition strength. Claims marked ⚠️ came from sources the verification pass couldn't finish
checking — treat them as leads, and re-verify before asserting them inside a published post.

### What we CAN win (this is the whole plan)

1. **The Pomene long tail is nearly empty.** The informational competitors are: a stale portal page
   frozen at ©2014 with "more information coming soon" stubs (✓ 2-1 verified), a low-authority
   selfdrive4x4.co.za page, one 2019 personal trip report, and one backpacker blog. All beatable on
   depth and freshness.
2. **Nobody ties SA→Mozambique logistics to Pomene specifically.** Border docs, third-party insurance,
   meticais, fuel, supply runs — covered generically by accommodationmozambique.co.za and
   mozambique.co.za, but **zero pages connect Komatipoort → EN1 → Massinga turnoff → Pomene**. This is
   the highest-intent gap and it's wide open.
3. **The best answers are locked inside Facebook.** The DriveMoz group + Zello is where real route/
   tide/road intel lives (✓ 3-0 verified that this is the community travellers actually use) — none of
   it is indexable by Google. A blog that publishes those answers has no organic competition.
4. **Pomene is missing from "best beaches in Mozambique" content.** A 2026 portal listicle features
   Bazaruto, Vilanculos, Tofo/Barra, Ponta do Ouro etc. and omits Pomene entirely (⚠️ unverified) —
   the comparison/discovery cluster is under-served.
5. **Direct competitor content is thin.** Pomene Hideaway — a near-identical product (4x4-only,
   off-grid, solar, self-catering, 5 en-suite rooms, ✓ 3-0 verified) — has good access copy but no
   blog depth on borders/insurance/supply runs. First mover on logistics content wins the niche.

trip reports (use these in H2s, FAQs, and body copy):

- "DriveMoz" · "Zello channel" · "cross-border letter" · "car not in your name affidavit"

## 3. Audit of the 3 existing posts

Overall: content quality is strong, structure (key takeaways, FAQ, internal links) is right.
The problems are targeting hygiene, not writing.

### `pomene-travel-guide.md` — the hub ✅ keep as flagship
- **De-facto primary:** "Pomene travel guide" / "Pomene Mozambique" — correct; title format (keyword + year + benefit) is good.
- **Issue — keyword overlap:** its frontmatter claims `how to get to Pomene` and `Vilankulo to Pomene`, which the 4x4 post also claims. Since both posts discuss the drive, Google may rank the hub for drive queries instead of the dedicated guide.
  **Fix:** remove those two from this post's list; let the 4x4 post own the drive cluster exclusively. Keep the internal link (already present) — that's the right mechanism.
- **Opportunity:** "things to do in Pomene" currently points to a homepage anchor; later, a dedicated "Things to do in Pomene" post can own that query (backlog).

### `best-time-to-visit-pomene.md` ✅ cleanest targeting of the three
- **Primary:** "best time to visit Pomene" — in title, slug, FAQ. Good.
- **Issue:** `Mozambique whale watching` in its keyword list — this post can't do double duty for a country-level whale query. The July whale post takes that; this post keeps "Pomene whale season" only.
- **Opportunity:** competitor framing says green-season (Nov–Mar) has cyclone risk in the south ⚠️ — the post currently says "rain in short bursts." Worth a candid line when verified; honesty builds the trust the drive-guide post already trades on.

### `drive-to-pomene-4x4-guide.md` ✅ right post, sharpen with forum vocabulary
- **Primary:** "do you need a 4x4 for Pomene" (question-format title — good for PAA).
- **Add the vocabulary searchers use:** the **Massinga turnoff** ("4km past Massinga, big Pomene/Morungulo sign"), the **boom gate** tyre-deflation ritual, "last 50km can take 2–3 hours though GPS says under an hour," and a mention of **DriveMoz/Zello** for live road intel. These are the exact phrases in forum threads and they're absent from the post.
- **Note:** post says 1.2–1.4 bar; forum consensus ranges 1.0–1.6 depending on load. Phrasing already hedges ("confirm for your vehicle") — fine.
- **Gap:** zero border/paperwork content — add a link to the July border post once live.

### Site-level findings (from the code audit)
1. **The `keywords:` frontmatter is decorative.** It renders only into `<meta name="keywords">` (ignored by Google since 2009) and JSON-LD `keywords` (no ranking weight). Keep it as an internal targeting record — but the plan's keywords must land in **title, description, H1/H2s, first paragraph, slug, and FAQ questions**, which is how this doc maps them.
2. **The FAQ block is the strongest SEO asset.** `app/journal/[slug]/page.tsx` renders real FAQPage
   structured data. Rule going forward: every FAQ `q:` should be a **verbatim search query** (see LT
   lines above).
3. **One-page-site ceiling.** All commercial sections are `/#anchors` on the homepage, so no dedicated
   page exists to rank for "Pomene accommodation"-type terms. Fine for now (those terms are conceded
   anyway), but if commercial rankings ever matter, sections need real URLs.
4. **Brand name is split three ways:** "Izmaan Lodge" (site.ts) vs "Iz-Ma-An Lodge" (all blog posts)
   vs "Izmaan Madelaine" (Google listing). Branded search is the highest-converting query a small
   lodge gets — standardise one spelling everywhere and keep the others as mentions ("also written
   Iz-Ma-An") on the homepage.
